\ann{RDY} (blue) & Steady: ready. Flashing: starting up or waiting for high voltage \\
\ann{RUN} (green) & Steady: running. Flashing: spinning up or down \\
\ann{FAULT} (red) & Steady: armed with no high voltage. Flashing: fault while armed \\
